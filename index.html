Concept of the day · nothing moved much today
Real yield 2.86%

The 10-year yield with expected inflation stripped out: what a lender actually earns after inflation.

Why it matters. It is the discount rate the whole market is priced against. Rising real yields make every future pound of profit worth less today, which presses on shares, gold and crypto alike.

The trap. A falling real yield is not automatically good news. It can fall because the market expects cuts into a weakening economy.
