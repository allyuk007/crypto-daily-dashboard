Concept of the day · from the rotation
Level, change and surprise

Three different questions: what is the number, how did it move, and was that what was expected?

Why it matters. Markets price expectations, so a reading only moves anything when it differs from what was already assumed.

The trap. Inflation at 3% is neither good nor bad until you know whether 2.6% or 3.4% was expected.
